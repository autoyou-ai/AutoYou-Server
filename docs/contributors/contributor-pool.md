# Contributor Pool

The Contributor Pool is a discretionary program through which OpenStorey LLC
("OpenStorey") shares part of the money it receives from sponsorships and
donations with the people who improve AutoYou Server. It is not an offer, a
contract, a wage, or a promise of payment.

You can sponsor AutoYou at [www.autoyou.me/donate](https://www.autoyou.me/donate/).
The pool is funded from the "Approved contributors" allocation that OpenStorey
commits to in the
[Open-Source Commitment](../legal/open-source-commitment.md).

## Current period

OpenStorey announces payment mechanics for each period. Until those mechanics
are announced, no individual award is promised. The commitment to allocate at
least 15% of qualifying net receipts continues to apply; unallocated or unpaid
amounts must remain accounted for in the funding ledger.

| Setting | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Period | Calendar quarter |
| Share of net sponsorship income set aside for the pool | The "Approved contributors" allocation in the public funding ledger (at least 15%) |
| Minimum payout | Not yet announced |
| Payment platform | Not yet announced |

"Net sponsorship income" means money OpenStorey actually receives, including
AutoYou Creator payments, through
[www.autoyou.me/donate](https://www.autoyou.me/donate/) and the links in
[`.github/FUNDING.yml`](../../.github/FUNDING.yml), after platform and payment
fees, taxes, refunds, and chargebacks. OpenStorey announces each
period's values before the period starts, in a pinned issue, and updates this
table.

**How this connects to the public funding ledger.** The pool is funded from the
"Approved contributors" allocation in the public funding ledger at
[www.autoyou.me/donate](https://www.autoyou.me/donate/#funding-status). Each
payout is recorded there as a contributor request whose public artifacts are the
merged pull requests, and the AutoYou account operator and then the OpenStorey
LLC board review it before it is paid. The ledger shows a display name only if
You choose one.

## Who can receive a payout

You can receive a payout for a period if all of these are true:

- One or more of Your Contributions were merged during the period.
- You accepted the [Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md) before the first of
  them was merged.
- You followed the [Code of Conduct](../../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
- Paying You is lawful, including under sanctions and export rules, and You give
  OpenStorey the payment details and tax forms it needs.
- OpenStorey is not already paying You for the same work.

## How points work

Maintainers give each merged Contribution points when it is merged:

| Points | Typical Contribution |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | A small bug fix, a test, or a documentation improvement |
| 3 | A feature, or a bug fix that touches several files |
| 8 | A major feature, a significant security fix, or a large refactor |

Maintainers may adjust points to reflect the real effort and value. These do not
earn points: whitespace, typo, or link-only changes; bulk or automated changes;
duplicates; Contributions reverted in the same period; and anything that broke
the rules in [CONTRIBUTING.md](../../CONTRIBUTING.md).

Points inform proposed awards; they are not a debt, wage, or fixed conversion
rate. Approved award calculations and any cap or minimum are announced for the
period. An approved amount below the announced minimum carries forward.

## Rules that keep the pool fair

- **Issue first.** Only Contributions linked to an issue that a maintainer
  approved and assigned to You earn points.
- **People only.** A person must review, understand, and take responsibility for
  every Contribution and disclose any AI tools used, as
  [CONTRIBUTING.md](../../CONTRIBUTING.md) explains. Output from an autonomous
  agent with no responsible person earns nothing.
- **One account per person.** Duplicate accounts, fake contributions, and
  attempts to inflate points lead to disqualification, and OpenStorey may
  reclaim payouts obtained that way.
- **Maintainers decide.** Points and payouts are at OpenStorey's discretion. You
  can ask for a review by opening an issue within 30 days of a period closing.
  The result is final.

## Payments and taxes

- OpenStorey pays through the platform it announces for the period. Platform and
  currency-conversion fees may reduce the amount You receive.
- You must give accurate payment details and any tax forms OpenStorey asks for
  (for example a W-9 or W-8). OpenStorey may withhold tax where the law requires
  it.
- You are responsible for Your own taxes.
- OpenStorey does not pay where the law prohibits it. In that case no amount is
  owed.
- OpenStorey aims to pay within 60 days after a period closes.

## Transparency

After each period, OpenStorey publishes the share it set aside, the total paid,
the number of contributors paid, and the total points earned. It does not name
anyone without their agreement.

## Changes

OpenStorey may change, pause, or end the program for future periods at any time.
A change does not withdraw an award already approved in writing or erase the
published allocation commitment for receipts already received. Statutory rights
and separate payment agreements remain unaffected.
